WHAT WORKS NOW

A guide for re Kiosk.

AI-assisted interpretation of English or Chinese questions when configured, a limited set of supported items, component separation, clarification questions, and links to published sources. Answers are displayed in English.

Questions may be sent to Alibaba Cloud to identify the item and its condition. Recommendations and citations come from the curated rule set. If AI is unavailable, the app labels its fallback to the English rule lookup. The app does not save query text; provider processing and retention follow the course service terms.

WHAT NEEDS MORE WORK

Careful about the gaps.

AI can misinterpret a description. Photo identification, general conversation, other collection points, and special collection events are outside this version.

It does not confirm current bin capacity, equipment status, or on-site labels. Hazardous waste is referred to specialist guidance.

How an answer is made

  1. 01Understand the item

    Interpret the item and its stated condition, or use the English rule lookup when AI is unavailable.

  2. 02Retrieve the rule

    Look up the curated re Kiosk rule and its conditions.

  3. 03Show the evidence

    Return a next step with its official source, or ask for clarification.
